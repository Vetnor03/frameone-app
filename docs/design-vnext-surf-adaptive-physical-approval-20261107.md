# Surf adaptive geometry — physical approval lock

Surf adaptive rendering is physically approved as of 2026-11-07.

## Locked anchor renderers

These existing Surf renderers remain unchanged:

- 2×2 — Medium
- 4×1 — Small
- 4×2 — Large
- 4×4 — XL

They are the visual anchors for every adaptive geometry.

## Approved adaptive rule set

The remaining 12 geometries use the physically approved R3 adaptive bridge:

- one-row geometries inherit the locked Small language
- 1×1 is deliberately minimal: rating, rating strip and wave range
- narrow portrait geometries keep one strong vertical hierarchy
- 2-column geometries inherit the locked Medium language
- narrow tall layouts show at most two dayparts to avoid collisions
- larger adaptive geometries use the Medium hero and spend extra room only on a clearly separated daypart/daily section
- 4×3 is the bridge between Large/XL without changing either anchor

No Surf data, scoring, cache, network, Today’s Best, experience-rating, direction-arrow, or refresh logic is changed by this lock.
